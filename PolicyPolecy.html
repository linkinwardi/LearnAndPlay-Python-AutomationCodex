Local data storage

This site stores only the information you enter directly in your browser: your name, selected language, chapter progress, quiz attempts, best score, and certificate status.

All of this data is saved locally in your browser. It remains on your device and is never transmitted to our servers.

No accounts, no tracking

The Automation Codex does not require a user account. We do not create user profiles or collect personal data for tracking.

Your study progress is only stored locally and is not linked to any backend database.

Third-party services

If analytics or advertising are enabled by the site operator, those third parties may process limited data such as your IP address, browser details, and usage patterns according to their own policies.

By default, this deployment ships without analytics or advertising enabled. If such services are added, display the required consent notice as needed.

Children

The site is designed for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

  • Clear your progress: use the Reset Progress button in the profile area or clear browser storage manually.
  • Control storage: use your browser privacy settings to block local storage or cookies if you prefer.
  • Keep data local: your learning progress remains under your control unless third-party services are added.
